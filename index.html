How it works

Three steps. No CSV wrangling, no tech headaches.

01

Paste your contacts

Dump your email list or phone numbers in any format — messy, mixed, unformatted. Blastwave cleans, deduplicates, and validates them automatically.

02

AI writes your messages

Type your idea and let AI generate unique message variations. Each recipient gets a slightly different version — better deliverability, higher open rates.

03

Send & watch results

Launch your campaign in one click. Track deliveries, failures, and engagement in real time on your dashboard.
